# Legal review checklist

The five policy pages are drafted, not approved. Nobody on the build side can
sign them off, and none of them has been.

While **Pages → [policy] → "Draft, requires legal review before publication"**
is ticked, the page shows a visible notice, is served `noindex` and is kept out
of the sitemap. It stays reachable, because the footer and the enquiry form's
consent checkbox link to it. Unticking the box does all three things at once.

Work through this list per page, then untick.

## Every policy

- [ ] The registered business name and legal entity are correct.
- [ ] The business address is correct, or deliberately omitted.
- [ ] The contact route for a legal or privacy question is correct and monitored.
- [ ] Governing law and jurisdiction are stated and correct for India.
- [ ] No clause promises something the business does not actually do.
- [ ] Dates and version wording are current.
- [ ] Nothing contradicts another policy.

## Privacy Policy

- [ ] Every category of personal data the enquiry form collects is listed: name, WhatsApp number, email, city, state, PIN code, requirement text and uploaded reference images.
- [ ] The lawful basis for each purpose is stated.
- [ ] Retention periods are real and achievable.
- [ ] Third parties that receive data are named: the hosting provider, the database, email delivery and, once configured, the analytics provider.
- [ ] Data subject rights and how to exercise them are described.
- [ ] Compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 has been assessed.
- [ ] If any processing happens outside India, that transfer is covered.

## Terms and Conditions

- [ ] It is unambiguous that submitting a requirement is an enquiry, not an order.
- [ ] It is unambiguous that Finquiry does not hold stock and does not guarantee availability.
- [ ] Pricing is described as per-specimen and quoted individually, never as a published price list.
- [ ] Payment terms, refunds and cancellation are stated.
- [ ] Liability limits are enforceable, and live-animal risk is addressed specifically.
- [ ] The point at which risk passes to the customer is defined.

## Cookie Policy

- [ ] Every cookie set is listed, with its purpose and lifetime.
- [ ] The consent cookie itself is included.
- [ ] The description matches what the site actually does: analytics are off until accepted, and nothing optional loads before a decision.
- [ ] Rejecting is described as being exactly as easy as accepting, which is how the banner behaves.
- [ ] The route to change a decision later is described, and it works: the footer's cookie preferences control.

## Sourcing and Delivery Policy

- [ ] The sourcing process matches how the business actually runs.
- [ ] Nothing implies a stock list, a catalogue or guaranteed availability.
- [ ] Transport, packing and handling responsibilities are assigned.
- [ ] What happens if a specimen becomes unavailable after approval is described.
- [ ] Any live-arrival assurance offered is one the business can honour.
- [ ] Interstate transport documentation requirements are accurate.

## Restricted-Species Policy

This one needs the closest reading. Wording here can create legal exposure.

- [ ] The policy states plainly that Finquiry does not source prohibited, protected or undocumented species.
- [ ] No wording implies restricted species can be obtained "on request", "with the right paperwork", "case by case" or through any similar opening.
- [ ] The Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972 and its schedules are referenced correctly.
- [ ] CITES obligations are described correctly where they apply.
- [ ] Any import requirements, including quarantine and permits, are accurate.
- [ ] The customer's own obligation to hold required permits is stated.
- [ ] The right to refuse a request is reserved unambiguously.
- [ ] No species is named as available in a way that could be read as an offer.
